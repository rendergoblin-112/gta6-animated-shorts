# Rockstar-safe rules (no exceptions)

These shorts are fan-made comedy about *wanting to play* GTA 6. They must not look like, or pass for, Rockstar material. Follow every rule.

## Never

- **No GTA logo art**, no "VI" Roman-numeral lettering, no logo-shaped badge, no recreation of the box art, key art or loading screens.
- **No Pricedown-style font** (the heavy GTA title face). Use plain hand lettering: the engine uses Patrick Hand and Permanent Marker (Google Fonts, OFL).
- **No Rockstar characters**: not Jason, not Lucia, not anyone from the trailers or earlier games. No look-alikes, no outfits that copy them.
- **No in-game place names**: no "Vice City", no "Leonida", no real in-game landmarks.
- **No game footage, screenshots, trailer frames, game music or trailer audio.** No sampling, no "inspired-by" remakes of trailer music.
- **No Rockstar or Take-Two branding**: no R* logo, no publisher marks, no rating-box mock-ups.
- **No other creators' material**: no frames, characters, designs, signature props, staging or audio from other channels' shorts. Your characters, your colours, your staging.

## Allowed

- The plain words **"GTA 6"** hand-lettered on the TV/phone screen (title, menu, "MISSION START").
- **Your own simple sunny game world** built with the engine: flat palms, pastel buildings, a road, a car, a minimap circle, a cash counter. Generic, no landmarks, no names.
- Generic game UI words: MISSION START, MISSION COMPLETE, PAUSED, LOADING, GAME OVER, PRESS START, PLAYER 2.
- Your own original sound effects (ElevenLabs prompts written in your own words, or the synthesized kit).

## Before publishing

- Title the short as a joke about the player ("When you…"), not as official content. Never claim to be or to show the real game.
- Check every screen shot: no logo shapes, no VI, no place names.
- Check every sound: nothing sampled from a trailer, the game, a meme clip or a song.
- Using the words "GTA 6" in a title is a trademark judgement you make; the skill keeps the visuals generic so the short does not depend on it.
